## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, institutional, yet deeply accessible digital sanctuary for legal empowerment. Embodying constitutional-grade gravity paired with intuitive civic utility, the visual identity balances formal legal tradition with the clarity required for high-stakes citizen interaction.

The style unites classic editorial structure with precise modern interface standards:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, unshakeable, clear, and reassuring.
- **Visual Stance:** Rigorous structural alignment reminiscent of constitutional charters, softened by purposeful warmth and unambiguous affordances.
- **Architectural Motifs:** Slender framing, stately serifs for pronouncements, clean geometric sans for dense legal literacy, and deliberate tri-color civic accents applied with strict editorial restraint.

## Colors

The palette establishes institutional credibility through deep constitutional blues, accented by vigilant warm gold and restorative civic teal.

### Primary & Core Surfaces
- **Primary Sovereign Navy (`#0D1754`):** The foundational anchor representing statutory authority and institutional weight. Applied to high-order structural frames, primary app bars, and dominant headlines.
- **Primary Mid Navy (`#1A237E`):** Used for interactive states and elevated dark surfaces.
- **Primary Tint Surface (`#E8EAF6`):** Soft, structural backing for quote containers and statutory references.
- **Accent Gold (`#F9A825`):** Reserved for imperative actions, urgent CTAs, and active navigation indicators. Deepened to `#F57F17` for pressed or high-contrast states.
- **Secondary Teal (`#00897B`):** Dedicated to public legal aid, restorative services, and citizen guidance pathways (backed by tint `#E0F2F1`).
- **Canvas Base (`#F5F7FF`):** Cool, glare-free tinted parchment grounding mobile reading under intense ambient light.
- **Pure Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity foreground for cards and reading planes.

### Semantic & Domain Indicators
- **Text Primary (`#1A1A2E`):** High-contrast, near-black ink for maximum legibility.
- **Text Secondary (`#555577`):** Muted structural text and metadata.
- **Divider & Border (`#E0E0EE`):** Architectural boundary lines.
- **Rights Categorization:** Arrest (`#B71C1C`), Bail (`#1B5E20`), Search (`#0D47A1`), Custody (`#4A148C`), Women (`#880E4F`), Consumer (`#E65100`).
- **Civic Accent Ribbon:** A micro 3px tricolor motif composed of `#FF9933`, `#FFFFFF`, and `#138808` to mark official constitutional decrees.

## Typography

The typographical pairing expresses constitutional legitimacy through classical press styling juxtaposed with pristine technical engineering.

- **Newsreader (Headlines & Formal Decrees):** Derived from classical editorial traditions, this serif confers unassailable weight, deliberate cadence, and legal provenance to statutory headings, rights declarations, and major interface titles.
- **Manrope (Body, UI, Data Display):** A geometric sans-serif engineered for maximum optical legibility at high densities. Its geometric letterforms ensure zero ambiguity during critical legal consumption, document drafting, and emergency procedure reviews.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a disciplined 4px base spatial unit, ensuring robust mobile-first alignment while handling complex procedural steps and legal transcripts.

- **Mobile Rhythm:** A 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` (16px) margins and gutters, creating maximum touch surfaces and comfortable reading widths without horizontal crowding.
- **Vertical Spacing:** Standard sections respect strict vertical increments: `space-xs` (4px) for tight label-to-input linkage, `space-sm` (8px) for list element separation, `space-md` (16px) for intra-card content, and `space-xl` (32px) for statutory document segmentation.
- **Adaptive Breakpoints:** Transitions gracefully from mobile single-column priority feeds to dual-pane legal case study views at 768px (tablet) and 1024px (desktop), scaling margins to `2rem` and `3rem` respectively.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating, generic software aesthetics in favor of architectural grounding and structural stability. Depth is established via crisp border structures paired with deep navy-tinted ambient diffusion.

- **Structural Outlines:** Every card, container, and actionable panel uses a baseline `1px` structural boundary tinted at `#E0E0EE`. This provides tangible containment even in low-contrast ambient environments.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Query Rows):** `0px 2px 8px rgba(13, 23, 84, 0.06)`, providing subtle surface lifting.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Modules, Floating Controls):** `0px 4px 16px rgba(13, 23, 84, 0.10)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Bottom Sheets, Critical Overlays):** `0px 12px 32px rgba(13, 23, 84, 0.16)`.
- **Active Accents:** Active interactive elements use a sharp, zero-spread ring: `0 0 0 2px #0D1754` with a `2px` white offset to maintain uncompromising accessibility.

## Shapes

In harmony with the constitutional charter motif, the shape language maintains crisp, formal architectural framing over informal, bubbly radii:

- **Baseline Geometry (`roundedness: 1`):** Structural elements adopt a compact, dignified `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px) curve, evoking the formal trimmed edges of legal paper stock.
- **Framed Cards & Data Panels:** Formed with controlled `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem`) contours, reinforcing precision, document-like reliability, and stability.
- **Action Elements & Status Badges:** System chips and compact action triggers utilize sharp, precise corners (`0.25rem`) to communicate functional sobriety and constitutional authority.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Statutory Authority):** Filled deep navy (`#0D1754`) with crisp text in white; height 48px; border-radius `0.25rem`; typography `label-lg`. Focus ring: 2px offset.
- **Urgent / Imperative CTA:** Accent Gold (`#F9A825`) with deep navy text (`#0D1754`); height 48px; bold typographic weight for rapid visual acquisition during urgent citizen scenarios.
- **Secondary / Aid Action:** Subtle teal tint (`#E0F2F1`) background with `#00897B` text and fine `1px` border.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Structural, boxed containers with `0.25rem` radius, bordered in `#E0E0EE` and filled with `#FFFFFF`.
- Active focus state sharpens the frame to 1.5px solid `#0D1754`.
- Integrated assistive labels positioned above the frame in `label-sm` using secondary neutral (`#555577`).

### Legal Status Badges & Rights Chips
- Compact, disciplined markers bounded by 1px solid borders corresponding to the category color (e.g., Arrest: `#B71C1C` on `#FFEBEE`).
- Squared geometry with soft `0.25rem` rounding to reflect formal stamps or seals.

### Constitutional Cards & Feed Tiles
- Grounded white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px `#E0E0EE` perimeter.
- Top-anchored optional civic ribbon: 3px height tricolor (`#FF9933`, `#FFFFFF`, `#138808`) for constitutional articles and foundational legal rights.
- Generous internal padding using `space-md` (16px).

### Bottom Navigation Dock
- Rigid 56px height surface fixed to the bottom viewport.
- Pure white fill with a clean 1px border-top (`#E0E0EE`) and ambient elevation Level 3.
- Five equidistant touch zones (48px minimum target size) supporting: Home, FIR, Chat, Rights, and Search.
- Active state highlighted by a 2px top stroke in Accent Gold (`#F9A825`) accompanied by bolded primary navy iconography.
