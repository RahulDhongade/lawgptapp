What are my rights if I am arrested by police in India?
09:42 AM done_all
balance
Legal Engine Breakdown Just now
overview Summary

Under Indian Law (BNSS 2023 & Article 22), police must inform you of the grounds of arrest, allow you to contact a lawyer or family, and mandatorily present you before a Judicial Magistrate within 24 hours.

gavel Relevant Statutory Law
check_circle Verified Source
Section 47 & 50 BNSS 2023 (replacing Sec 50 & 57 CrPC) Article 22(1) & (2), Constitution of India
menu_book Procedural Explanation
  • 1
    Right to Know Grounds Must be informed immediately of specific allegations and whether the offense is bailable.
  • 2
    Right to Counsel Allowed to consult and be defended by a legal practitioner of your choice during interrogation.
  • 3
    Free Legal Aid State-mandated legal representation if indigent or unable to engage a private attorney.
  • 4
    Protection for Women No female can be arrested after sunset and before sunrise except with prior Judicial Magistrate sanction and a female officer.
task_alt What You Can Do Immediately

• Demand an Arrest Memo recording exact time, location, and signed by at least one respectable local witness or family member.
• Request an immediate statutory medical examination under Section 53 BNSS to record physical state.

warning
Crucial Directive Do not sign blank papers under duress. Maintain composure and insist on your right to telephone your family or an advocate immediately.
